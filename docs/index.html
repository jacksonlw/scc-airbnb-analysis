Analysis of Airbnb Listings in Santa Clara County, California (as of 23 December, 2024)

By Arshia Mathur, Jackson Lawrence, and Nikhil Mandava

Our Goal

We aim to identify the key characteristics of high-performing Airbnb properties in Santa Clara County. By analyzing key features like host responsiveness, pricing, and more, we hope to discover what makes a listing successful in the area. We aimed to create a variety of visualizations to illustrate the performance of Airbnb listings within the region. Each visualization highlights different characteristics that contribute to what makes an Airbnb high-performing.


Data Introduction

We are using data from InsideAirbnb, a project with a mission to provide data and induce advocacy about Airbnb's impact on residential communities. Although the data is already cleaned, verified, analyzed, and aggregated, we performed additional data cleaning to ensure we analyzed the data relevant to our hypothesis. We created a “performance_score” variable to represent the combined effect of price competitiveness and availability, allowing us to compare listings within the same room type on a standardized scale. Furthermore, we eliminated outliers and incomplete entries to guarantee that the dataset was clean, uniform, and suitable for analysis. The size of the data is 4,600, as we will be reviewing 4,600 Airbnb listings. The data includes a mix of categorical, numerical, and continuous values.

Analysis


Relationship Between Room Type and Price

The box plot shows that Entire home/apartment listings dominate the higher price range, with many exceeding $1000 and some nearing $2800, and display the widest variation, reflecting diverse property types. Their median price falls between $200 and $300. Hotel rooms have a tighter, more consistent price range, mostly between $250 and $750. Private rooms are typically under $200 but can occasionally reach $1000, indicating a few premium options. Shared rooms are the most affordable and consistent, clustering below $100, appealing to budget-conscious travelers.

Validating Performance Score Using Price and Availability

This scatter plot is used to validate that our performance score accurately reflects listing success by related price per night to nights available. Each point is a single listing with color representing the performance score (lighter colors = higher scores). Since high-performing listings should have higher prices and be booked more often—lower availability—we expect lighter-colored points to appear in the lower-right region. That's exactly what the chart shows: listings with lower availability and higher prices are clustered in the bottom-right and are lighter in color, while the darker-colored listings (lower scores) are mostly in the upper-left, where availability is high and price is low. This confirms that our scoring method aligns well with our definition of performance and reflects patterns in the actual data.

Average Ratings by Performance Tier

Performance Tiers by score: High (80-100), Medium (50-79), Low (0-49)

This bar chart shows how guest ratings differ across high, medium, and low performing Airbnbs, these tiers are determined by our calculated performance score. Each bar represents the average rating in a specific category like cleanliness, communication, or value. Listings in the high-performance tier consistently have higher ratings across all categories, while medium and low tier listings show slightly lower scores. This pattern helps confirm that our performance score is working as intended. It's not just based on price and availability, but also generally lines up with how satisfied guests are. Even though the differences in ratings are small, since most listings are rated pretty highly, the trend is clear: better-performing listings correlate with higher review scores in all categories.

Performance Score Distribution by Amenity Type

The chart showing the performance score distribution for each amenity type reveals that the median performance scores for most amenities cluster around the 50th percentile. This suggests that many amenities are common across Airbnb listings and therefore do not significantly differentiate high-performing properties. Notably, amenities such as Scenic View and Family Friendly exhibit a slightly higher median score of ~55%, indicating these amenities may contribute more positively to performance. On the other hand, widely available amenities like Essentials, Kitchen, and Connectivity show similar distributions. While these kinds of amenities are necessary, they are not significant enough to drive higher performance. This analysis suggests that while basic amenities are foundational, it is the more unique or desirable features—like scenic views or family-oriented setups—that may help increase a listing's success.
