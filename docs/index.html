Analysis of Airbnb Listings in Santa Clara County, California (as of 23 December, 2024)

By Arshia Mathur, Jackson Lawrence, and Nikhil Mandava

Our Goal

We aim to identify the key characteristics of high-performing Airbnb properties in Santa Clara County. By analyzing key features like host responsiveness, pricing, and more, we hope to discover what makes a listing successful in the area. We aimed to create a variety of visualizations to illustrate the performance of Airbnb listings within the region. Each visualization highlights different characteristics that contribute to what makes an Airbnb high-performing.


Data Introduction

We are using data from InsideAirbnb, a project with a mission to provide data and induce advocacy about Airbnb's impact on residential communities. Although the data is already cleaned, verified, analyzed, and aggregated, we performed additional data cleaning to ensure we analyzed the data relevant to our hypothesis. We created a “performance_score” variable to represent the combined effect of price competitiveness and availability, allowing us to compare listings within the same room type on a standardized scale. Furthermore, we eliminated outliers and incomplete entries to guarantee that the dataset was clean, uniform, and suitable for analysis. The size of the data is 4,600, as we will be reviewing 4,600 Airbnb listings. The data includes a mix of categorical, numerical, and continuous values. This data can be found in other research projects, such as Value for Money and Selection: How Pricing Affects Airbnb Ratings and Airbnb listings' performance: determinants and predictive models

Analysis

Airbnb Performance Score Map in the Bay Area

This interactive map offers a geographical perspective on Airbnb performance scores across the Bay Area, helping us explore which attributes may influence higher pricing and success. By mapping each listing and color-coding it based on their performance score, we can visually identify regional patterns to support our hypothesis that certain location-based factors could contribute to stronger Airbnb performance. Notably, we observed a cluster of high-performing Airbnbs in the Palo Alto area- an affluent region with high cost of living and proximity to major tech hubs- suggesting that demand and local wealth could play a role in driving performance. In contrast, lower-performing listings are more concentrated in East San Jose, a relatively less expensive area, which may reflect lower nightly rates or reduced demand. The visualization also offers a Tooltip feature, allowing us to hover over individual dots to discover the AirBnb’s name, performance score, review rating, and number of reviews. We observed that although some Airbnbs were lower performing, they had higher ratings. However, these higher ratings were often because the overall number of ratings were low, such as a 2 or 1. In this way, this spatial analysis adds valuable context to our broader investigation of what drives Airbnb success.

Interactive Visualization

You can explore the map by clicking and dragging to move around Santa Clara County, California. Use your mouse or trackpad to zoom in and out for a closer look at specific neighborhoods. Hover over individual dots to see listing details like performance score, rating, and number of reviews.

Relationship Between Room Type and Price

The box plot shows that Entire home/apartment listings dominate the higher price range, with many exceeding $1000 and some nearing $2800, and display the widest variation, reflecting diverse property types. Their median price falls between $200 and $300. Hotel rooms have a tighter, more consistent price range, mostly between $250 and $750. Private rooms are typically under $200 but can occasionally reach $1000, indicating a few premium options. Shared rooms are the most affordable and consistent, clustering below $100, appealing to budget-conscious travelers.

Validating Performance Score Using Price and Availability

This scatter plot is used to validate that our performance score accurately reflects listing success by related price per night to nights available. Each point is a single listing with color representing the performance score. Since high-performing listings should have higher prices and be booked more often—lower availability—we expect dark red colored points to appear in the lower-right region. That's exactly what the chart shows: listings with lower availability and higher prices are clustered in the bottom-right and are darker in color, while the lighter, yellow-colored listings (lower scores) are mostly in the upper-left, where availability is high and price is low. This confirms that our scoring method aligns well with our definition of performance and reflects patterns in the actual data.

Average Review Ratings by Performance Tier

Performance Tiers by score: High (80-100), Medium (50-79), Low (0-49)

This bar chart shows how guest ratings differ across high, medium, and low performing Airbnbs, these tiers are determined by our calculated performance score. Each bar represents the average rating in a specific category like cleanliness, communication, or value. Listings in the high-performance tier consistently have higher ratings across all categories, while medium and low tier listings show slightly lower scores. This pattern helps confirm that our performance score is working as intended. It's not just based on price and availability, but also generally lines up with how satisfied guests are. Even though the differences in ratings are small, since most listings are rated pretty highly, the trend is clear: better-performing listings correlate with higher review scores in all categories.

Interactive Visualization

Click on any bar to highlight that review category across all performance tiers. This lets you easily compare how categories like Cleanliness, Communication, or Check-in are rated for high, medium, and low-performing listings.

Performance Score Distribution by Amenity Type

The chart showing the performance score distribution for each amenity type reveals that the median performance scores for most amenities cluster around the 50th percentile. This suggests that many amenities are common across Airbnb listings and therefore do not significantly differentiate high-performing properties. Notably, amenities such as Scenic View and Family Friendly exhibit a slightly higher median score of ~55%, indicating these amenities may contribute more positively to performance. On the other hand, widely available amenities like Essentials, Kitchen, and Connectivity show similar distributions. While these kinds of amenities are necessary, they are not significant enough to drive higher performance. This analysis suggests that while basic amenities are foundational, it is the more unique or desirable features—like scenic views or family-oriented setups—that may help increase a listing's success.

Summary & Findings

Through our analysis of Airbnb listings in the Santa Clara county, we aimed to understand which key characteristics would contribute to high-performing properties. By leveraging data from InsideAirbnb, we examined various factors, and identified some key findings.

What We Discoverd

From our analysis, we discovered: Entire homes/apartments dominate the higher price tiers, which is likely due to travelers looking for a wider range of amenities, privacy, and space. They had the widest price range, with some listings up to $1000 and a median of $200 - 300. This is compared to hotel rooms (ranging from $250 - 750), private rooms (under $200), and shared rooms (under $100).

Performance scores are positively correlated with both higher prices and lower availability, validating our scoring method. High-scoring listings are often more expensive and frequently booked, which was demonstrated in our scatterplot.

High-performing listings consistently receive better guest reviews, especially in categories like cleanliness, communication, and value. This trend indicates a correlation between performance scores and guest satisfaction.

Common amenities do not significantly impact performance scores, since the median performance scores for amenities clustered around the 50th percentile. However, amenities like 'Scenic View' and 'Family Friendly' exhibited slightly higher median scores (~55%), indicating a potential positive impact on performance.

Location matters, as our interactive map demonstrated that higher performing Airbnbs were concentrated in areas with a greater cost of living and affluence, while lower performing Airbnbs were concentrated in the opposite areas.

Future Work

In the future, we hope to expand our project and test our hypothesis in several different ways. Some next steps could be:

1. Analyzing Listing Dates- we could explore the impact between the length of time an Airbnb has been listed versus its number of reviews and overall performance score. This could be important for listings that may have less reviews not because they are undesirable options, but rather since they have been listed for less time.

2. Analyzing Metropolitan Cities- we could compare patterns and test our current findings against urban areas like San Francisco to see if similar factors drive performance or if city-specific dynamics play a larger role.

3. Integrating Local Attractions- by integrating APIs like Google Maps, we could test to see if popular restaurants or tourist destinations impact pricing or Airbnb success.

4. Analyzing Seasonality- through time-series data, we could see whether certain times of the year (summer, fire season, tech conferences, winter holidays) impact the availability, pricing, reviews, and overall performance of Airbnbs.
